What to include in your message

Story suggestions

Share the full title, any alternate title you know, and what interests you about the story. If available, include a link to the official publisher or licensed reading page. A suggestion does not guarantee a future video.

Corrections or website problems

Include the page URL or video link, the paragraph or timestamp involved, and a short explanation of the issue. For a factual correction, a source link helps identify what needs checking. For a display problem, mention your device and browser and attach a screenshot if useful.

Collaborations and business inquiries

Describe your project, who you represent, the proposed work, and your expected timeline. Include a budget or compensation details if applicable, along with a reply address.

Questions about referenced material

If your inquiry concerns material you created or represent, email the specific page or video URL, identify the material, and explain your connection to it and the action you are requesting. Please send contact details privately rather than posting them in public comments.

Before sending

Keep your message focused on the information needed to handle your inquiry. Do not include passwords, financial account details, or identity documents. This page has no submission form; contact is through your email service or YouTube.
