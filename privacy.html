Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes information handling on the Monarchs Library website at jhomguanzon14-ui.github.io. The website provides fantasy reading guides and information about the Monarchs Library YouTube channel. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the website operator.

Information you choose to send

You do not need an account to read this website. The site does not provide an account registration or contact submission form. If you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown with your message, its contents, and any attachments you send. We use that information to handle your inquiry, such as responding to a question, reviewing a correction, or discussing a collaboration.

Contact email is handled through Gmail and the email provider you use to send your message. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment account details, or identity documents.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. This processing occurs as part of hosting the website. See GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Google advertising and similar technologies

Some pages include Google AdSense code. Including that code does not mean advertisements are always displayed. Where Google advertising services operate, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies and similar technologies to provide advertising and related services.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize advertisements using those visits. Advertising services may also process IP addresses, browser and device information, and interactions with advertisements, and may use web beacons or similar identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Learn more about this processing at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your advertising and cookie choices

  • Visit Google My Ad Center to manage Google's personalized advertising settings.
  • Visit the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page for choices offered by participating advertising companies.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. These controls vary by browser, and blocking cookies may affect third-party features.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily stop advertisements or all information processing. Some settings are specific to an account, browser, or device. These external controls are not a substitute for any consent required before advertising technologies are used.

YouTube and other external links

The website links to YouTube and the Monarchs Library YouTube channel. Following a link takes you to a separate service whose own privacy practices apply. Comments posted on YouTube may be public and are handled by YouTube under Google's policies. The website uses links to the channel rather than an embedded YouTube player.

Other external websites linked from our guides or information pages handle information according to their own policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Privacy questions and requests

To ask about personal information you have sent us, or request a correction or deletion, email jhom.guanzon14@gmail.com. Describe the relevant message or interaction so it can be identified, without adding unnecessary sensitive information. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location and circumstances.

Requests about information held independently by GitHub, Google, YouTube, or your email provider should also be directed to that service through its privacy controls or contact channels. Their policies explain their storage practices and retention of service data.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our contact email, let us know so the matter can be reviewed and the information removed as appropriate.

Policy updates

This page may be updated when the website or its information handling changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email jhom.guanzon14@gmail.com. You can also find contact instructions on our Contact page.
